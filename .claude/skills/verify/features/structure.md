# Project structure

A project's home at `/projects/<id>` shows its structure: a card per tower when there are several, the tower's façade directly when there is one, and the project's common areas. A tower at `/projects/<id>/locations/<towerId>` reads as its façade, one floor per row with the highest on top, units in columns aligned on the widest floor and common areas in a column of their own. A floor lists its units, each with its typology and changes, and its common areas; a unit or common area opens its typology and its rooms. A blank project offers `Montar estrutura`, the wizard at `/projects/<id>/structure/new`: on a desktop one page with the towers, the common areas, the tipologias and a preview pinned 24px from the viewport top, fitting within the viewport minus 48px with only its tower list scrolling (when its alerts leave the tower list under 180px, the whole preview scrolls instead); on a phone four steps with a bottom bar. Nothing is saved before `Criar estrutura`, which sends one request that creates the tipologias with their rooms and the towers, floors, units and common areas of the plan; each unit is born with the rooms of the tipologia its rules give it. Anyone linked to an in-progress project (and the organization's admin) adds and renames in place: `Adicionar` on the home, `Adicionar unidade` and `Adicionar área comum` at the end of a floor's lists, `Adicionar área comum` on a tower, and an ellipsis menu per row and per page with `Renomear`, `Mover para cima` / `Mover para baixo` (floors and units), `Duplicar` (towers, floors and units) and `Remover`. A unit and a common area edit their rooms in place and change or return to their typology. Every location page has a `Disciplinas` card with one checkbox per specific discipline of the project, saved the moment it is ticked, `Nova disciplina`, which creates one for the whole project or only where it is turned on, and a button that applies disciplines in batch to many locations at once. The configuration page at `/projects/<id>/configuration` sums the structure up and edits the project's tipologias. A delivered project is read-only for every role: no add row and no menu, and the lock line `A obra foi entregue: a estrutura não pode mais ser alterada.`.

## Sub-features

- `home-empty` shows `A obra ainda não tem estrutura` with `Monte torres, andares, unidades e áreas comuns de uma vez. Depois, tudo se ajusta na fachada.`, the link `Montar estrutura` and the ghost button `Adicionar uma torre por vez`, which turns into the `Nome da torre` field prefilled `Torre A` (E1); a delivered project without structure shows `A estrutura aparece aqui quando alguém da equipe montar a obra.` under the lock line and no link.
- `wizard-steps` opens `Montar estrutura` with the project name as back link and `<project> · nada é salvo antes de criar`: `Torres e andares` (towers stepper; per tower `Faixas de andares`, one card `Faixa 1`, `Faixa 2`… per range with `Primeiro andar`, `Último andar`, `Finais` (`Ex.: 01, 02`, `Ex.: A, B` in letters, help `Separe os finais com vírgula e espaço.`) and the box `Andares sem unidades` (hides `Finais`), a count line `18 unidades · 9 andares · finais 01 e 02` / `0 unidades · 1 andar` / `Preencha a faixa para ver as unidades.`, `Tirar a faixa 2` with more than one, the ghost `Faixa` that starts the next range on the floor after the last one, and `Total: 25 unidades em 16 andares` (`Torre A: …` with several towers); `Térreo sem unidades` checked, with `Tipologia do hall de entrada (opcional)` while it is, and `Hall em cada andar` unchecked at the start, with `Tipologia dos halls (opcional)` while it is (both offer `Sem tipologia`, the common-area tipologias and `Nova tipologia de área comum…`); numbering `101, 102` or `1A, 1B`, which converts the typed finals by position; with several towers each gets a `Nome` and a new one copies the previous ranges and ground floor with the next letter, without the hall), `Áreas comuns` (of the project, of each tower, of one floor on a floor some range has), `Tipologias` and `Prévia`; on a phone `Passo 1 de 4` with a bar `Continuar · <next step>` and `Voltar` (W1, W2, W2b).
- `wizard-ranges` turns the ranges into floors and units: the stacks are the interval from the lowest to the highest typed (`01, 04` is 01 to 04, `1, 3` is `01, 03`, `03, 04` only 03 and 04, `A, C` in letters is A to C), a unit is the floor plus the stack (`201`, `2A`), a floor exists only inside a range, a range without units makes floors with no unit (and the `Hall` when it is on). A tower with any invalid range builds nothing and counts nothing: its preview reads `Complete as faixas desta torre para ver os andares.` (`Preencha as faixas para ver os andares.` while blank) and its total `0 unidades em 0 andares`. Two ranges on one floor answer `O 10º andar já está na faixa 1. Cada andar fica numa faixa só.` in place of the count line, with the card ringed in red (W9); letters with more than 26 finals in a range are refused with `A faixa 2 da Torre A tem 28 finais. Com letras, o máximo é 26, de A a Z.` and nothing changes.
- `wizard-typologies` (step 3) starts with `Unidades` holding `Tipologia A` and `Áreas comuns` empty (`0 tipologias`, `Escolhidas no Hall em cada andar, no hall de entrada e em cada área comum.`). The step reads `A lista de cômodos de cada tipo, num catálogo para unidades e outro para áreas comuns. Uma tipologia de área comum só vale para área comum.` A draft card has `Nome da tipologia`, its room count, rooms one per row (`Tirar Sala`, empty `Sem cômodo.`), the suggest field `Novo cômodo` (typing `sa` lists `Sala` and `Sacada` under `Sugestões` and a room that exists only on a draft common-area tipologia under `Usados nesta obra`; leaving the field adds nothing, Enter adds; a repeat answers `Esse cômodo já está na tipologia.`) and `Copiar`. `Nova tipologia` / `Nova tipologia de área comum` open the name dialog (`Ex.: Tipologia D, Garden.` / `Ex.: Hall, Corredor, Garagem.`, `Os cômodos entram depois, no cartão da tipologia.`) and create nothing on the server. A name taken in either catalog, ignoring accents, case and repeated spaces, answers `Esse nome já existe nesta obra.` before sending. `Copiar` suggests `Tipologia A (cópia)`, cancel creates nothing, confirm adds a draft copy in the same catalog with the rooms the dialog showed, and taking a room off the original leaves the copy. A tipologia the obra already has is read-only (`Já existe na obra. Os cômodos mudam na Configuração.`, `Copiar`, no rename and no `Tirar`). Taking a draft tipologia out takes its rules and returns `Sem tipologia` where it was chosen. `Onde se aplica` is a list of rule cards, see `wizard-rules`.
- `wizard-rules` (W5, W5c) is `Onde se aplica`, a list of cards, one per rule, on a desktop and on a phone, with nothing typed: `Cada regra dá uma tipologia às unidades dos finais e andares escolhidos. Final que a torre não tem fica de fora, sem erro.` A card reads `Regra 1 · Tipologia A` and `18 unidades` (the units the rule reaches now; `1 unidade`, `0 unidades`), with `Tirar a regra 1`, the select `Tipologia da regra 1` (unit tipologias only, no `Sem tipologia`), `Torre da regra 1` only with several towers (one tower per rule, until the tower menu lands), the menu button `Finais da regra 1` showing `Todos os finais`, `03 e 04` or `Nenhum final`, and the selects `Do andar da regra 1` (`Primeiro` and the tower's floors) and `Até o andar da regra 1` (`Último` and the floors from `Do andar`). The menu `Finais da regra 1` lists `Todos os finais` and one checkbox per final the rule's tower has, stays open while the person ticks, and a final tapped with `Todos os finais` on leaves only that one; with no range filled it says `Preencha as faixas no passo 1 para escolher os finais.`. `Regra` adds a rule for the first tower, every final, open floors and the first unit tipologia, and the note `Unidade sem regra fica sem tipologia, e os cômodos dela se cadastram nela.` follows. A new tower starts with no rule; shortening a range (once the field is left, never mid-typing) or removing a tower takes what is gone out of the rules, and switching a rule's `Torre` drops the finals and floors the new tower lacks, and a rule left with `Nenhum final` or no tower shows `Escolha ao menos um final.` / `Escolha ao menos uma torre.` on its card and blocks `Criar estrutura` (the click goes to the rule and sends nothing), while a rule that reaches 0 units blocks nothing. Two rules that reach one unit, with the same tipologia or not, show in the preview the alert `Regras em conflito` / `Duas regras dão tipologia às mesmas unidades.` with the button `Ver regras` (on a phone it goes back to the Tipologias step and focuses the first rule); `Criar estrutura` is off, nothing is sent, and the units in conflict count in no tipologia and not in `Sem tipologia`.
- `wizard-preview` keeps the desktop app header scrolling with the page and the preview at 24px after scrolling, including at 1280×600; only its tower list scrolls, unless its alerts would squeeze the tower list under 180px, when the whole preview scrolls instead. It shows `1 torre · 4 andares · 12 unidades`, the common area count with the units of each tipologia (`5 áreas comuns · Tipologia A: 12`, `· Sem tipologia: 4`), a card `4 unidades ficam sem tipologia. Os cômodos delas se cadastram em cada uma.` when a rule leaves units out (W4), the reservation box `Esta estrutura usa 81 de 400 reservadas. Depois de criar, sobram 319.` (`sobra 1`) over its detail `61 unidades + 16 halls de andar + 2 halls de entrada + 2 outras áreas comuns` (parts that are zero left out; the reservation is the project's, what is left comes from the server), or `Complete as faixas para ver quanto da reserva esta estrutura usa.` while a tower is incomplete, and one small façade per tower, titled `Torre única` when there is one (W2).
- `wizard-validation` shows `Informe o primeiro andar.`, `Use um andar a partir do 1º.` (`2a`, `2.5`), `Informe o último andar.`, `O último andar precisa ser igual ou maior que o primeiro.`, `Informe os finais desta faixa.`, `Use números de 1 a 30, como 01, 02.` / `Use letras de A a Z, como A, B.`, `De 1 a 30 unidades por andar.`, `Informe o nome da tipologia.`, `Esse nome já existe nesta obra.`, `Escolha ao menos um final.`, `Escolha ao menos uma torre.` and the like under the fields (a field's message goes once it is edited; on a phone it returns to the step that holds the field), scrolls to the first wrong one (the range's `Primeiro andar` for a shared floor) and focuses it, and sends nothing (W9).
- `wizard-reserve` disables `Criar estrutura` when the plan's units, floor halls, entrance halls and other common areas are more than remain: the box drops `Depois de criar` (`Esta estrutura usa 81 de 400 reservadas.`) and the alert `Faltam 51 unidades na reserva` (`Falta 1 unidade na reserva`) reads `Esta estrutura usa 81 e a obra ainda tem 30.` (`… e a reserva da obra acabou.` with none left) with the button `Alterar reserva` for the admin, `Peça mais ao admin da construtora.` for a manager or assistant; on a phone the alert rides in the bottom bar above the buttons, and every step's end scrolls clear of the bar (W10, W10b).
- `wizard-create` creates the plan, returns to the project home with the toast `Estrutura criada: 1 torre e 12 unidades.` lasting 12 seconds, with the link `Ver tipologias` to `/projects/<id>/configuration#tipologias`, and the façade; a created unit opens its tipologia and rooms, and a common area with a tipologia and no rooms of its own is born with that tipologia’s rooms. While sending, controls lock and the button reads `Criando estrutura…` / `Adicionando andares…` (W11, A6). A lost or unreadable answer locks the form with `Não foi possível confirmar`, `Tentar de novo` and `Voltar a editar`; either checks the first location before proceeding, and retry reuses the ids (W12, A7). On reload a pending attempt is checked before editing (W13). `NO_UNITS_LEFT`, `LOCATION_NAME_TAKEN` and `TYPOLOGY_NAME_TAKEN` return to editing with their alerts. The lost-answer paths need the route-tree fake server, not a healthy instance.
- `wizard-draft` persists each edit in this browser, separately per account, project and flow. Reload shows `Rascunho restaurado` and `Descartar rascunho` (W14); the banner disappears on editing. Leaving within the app asks `Descartar o que você montou?`, while reload and session expiry retain the draft. A second tab's change locks the older tab with `Este rascunho mudou em outra aba` and `Carregar a versão mais recente`; sign-out or a different account deletes it.

- `wizard-delivered` shows the lock line and, in place of the wizard, the block `A obra foi entregue` / `A estrutura não pode mais ser alterada. Dá para conferir tudo na fachada e na configuração.` with the button `Voltar para a obra`, also on `?tower` (W15).
- `wizard-not-blank` sends `/projects/<id>/structure/new` on a project that already has structure to its home, replacing the history entry; after `Criar estrutura` the browser's Back does not reopen the wizard. `?tower=<id>` is the exception (`wizard-add-floors`); a `tower` that is not a tower of the project, or not an id, also lands on the home.
- `wizard-add-floors` (A1 to A5, A8, A9) opens at `/projects/<id>/structure/new?tower=<towerId>` from `Adicionar andares` (the outline button beside the page menu on a desktop tower, the item of `Ações da torre` on a phone and a desktop, the button beside `Adicionar` on a single-tower façade, the button of a tower or façade without floors, and `Assistente` / `Montar com o assistente`, which ask which tower when there are several): the band `Adicionar andares à Torre B` with the back link `Torre B` (`Adicionar andares` and the back link to the project with a single tower). Step 1 is `Novos andares`, described `Os andares novos entram na Torre B pela ordem do número, com as suas faixas e finais.`, with the subtitle `Andares novos` and `A Torre B tem hoje do 1º ao 10º andar. Use andares que ela ainda não tem.` (`A obra tem hoje …` with a single tower). Ranges are the same cards as Montar (`Faixa 1`, `Faixa`, `Andares sem unidades`), with `Do andar` prefilled with the next number after the highest numbered floor and `Último andar` and `Finais` empty, the total `Nesta adição: 2 unidades em 2 andares`, and `Hall em cada andar` starting checked when that highest floor has a `Hall`, described `Repete a escolha da Torre B. Desmarque para não criar os halls só nesta adição.`; no `Térreo`, tower count, tower name or numbering, and no common-area step. The tipologias stay: the project's own show as read-only cards, the rules start as the finals of the last floor (`01, 03` → `Tipologia A`), with no `Torre da regra` control, and `Nova tipologia` still adds one. The preview reads `Torre B ganha 2 andares e 2 unidades`, `Estes andares usam 4 das 50 que restam na reserva. Depois de adicionar, sobram 46.` over `2 unidades + 2 halls de andar`, a façade of only the new floors with a dashed border and the button `Adicionar 2 andares` (`Adicionar 1 andar`, `Adicionar andares` with none); the note is `Nada é salvo antes de adicionar. Depois, tudo se ajusta na fachada e na configuração.`. A floor the tower already has answers `A Torre B já tem o 9º andar.` and a new unit name it already has `A Torre A já tem a unidade 1301. Use outro andar ou outros finais.` (`A obra já tem a unidade 1301. Use outro andar ou outros finais.` with one tower), both on `Do andar` with nothing sent. A new floor is placed after the highest numbered floor below it, or before the lowest when none is below; `Térreo` and `Cobertura` stay where they are. Over the reserva it shows `Faltam 2 unidades na reserva` with `Estes andares usam 4 e a obra ainda tem 2.` (`… e a reserva da obra acabou.`) and disables the button (A5). Success returns to the tower (the home with a single tower) with the toast `2 andares adicionados à Torre B.` (`1 andar adicionado.`) (A8). On a phone there are three steps (`Novos andares`, `Tipologias`, `Prévia`); a delivered project shows the lock line and the `wizard-delivered` block (A9).
- `home-towers` shows `Torres` with a card per tower (Andares, Unidades, Áreas comuns, Tipologias: `Tipologia A, Tipologia B, Cobertura`, or `Sem tipologia`) and `Áreas comuns da obra` (E2); a delivered project adds the lock line (E5).
- `home-single-tower` shows `Fachada` with the outline `Adicionar andares` and the `Adicionar` menu, and `Áreas comuns` with the tower's and the project's common areas, and the tower's name nowhere (E3); a tower without floors shows `A obra ainda não tem andares` with the `Adicionar andares` button.
- `tower-facade` shows the façade, `Áreas comuns da torre`, the band `Torre · 15 andares · 50 unidades` and the back link to the project (T1); the desktop band has an outline `Adicionar andares` beside the page menu and the menu has the item too (T1, T2); a tower without floors shows `A Torre B ainda não tem andares`, `Adicione os andares e as unidades de uma vez, com a numeração pronta.` and the button `Adicionar andares` (T3); a delivered project adds the lock line (T6).
- `floor-lists` shows `Unidades`, each row with `Tipologia A`, `Tipologia A · 3 alterações` or `Sem tipologia`, `Áreas comuns do andar`, the band `Andar · Torre A · 4 unidades` and the back link to the tower (F1); with a single tower both drop the tower.
- `add-tower` adds one tower from `Adicionar uma torre por vez` (E1) or from the `Adicionar` menu of the home (`Torre` and `Área comum da obra`; E2, and E3 beside the façade): the field `Nome da torre` suggests the first free `Torre <letter>` (`Torre C` with two towers, `Torre B` with one), Enter adds, Escape cancels, and a single-tower project then shows both towers as cards. The menu also has `Montar com o assistente`, which goes to `wizard-add-floors` of the only tower or, with several, is a heading over one item per tower (`Adicionar andares à Torre A`, `… à Torre B`; not a menuitem itself).
- `add-unit` turns the last row of a floor's `Unidades` into the field `Adicionar unidade` with the next number prefilled (`905`; `1001` on an empty `10º andar`), the hint `Usa 1 das 12 unidades que restam nesta obra.` (`Usa 1 da única unidade que resta nesta obra.` with one), Enter adds at the end of the floor and Escape cancels (F2, F3); a taken name shows `Já existe a unidade 903 na Torre A.` in the field in place of the hint, `nesta torre` with a single tower (F4); a `NO_UNITS_LEFT` answer shows `A reserva da obra acabou.` in the field and the row turns into F5 once cancelled. A common area uses one of the reserva like a unit; floors and towers never use it.
- `add-common-area` adds `Adicionar área comum` to the obra (beside the façade on a single tower), a tower or a floor, with an empty field and the unit's reserva hint `Usa 1 das 12 unidades que restam nesta obra.` (`Usa 1 da única unidade que resta nesta obra.`) (L8); a taken name reads `Já existe uma área comum Hall neste andar.`.
- `add-reserve-over` keeps `Adicionar unidade` and every `Adicionar área comum` visible and disabled when no unit remains (L8, L8b), described by `A reserva da obra acabou. Fale com o admin da construtora.` for a manager or assistant and `A reserva da obra acabou.` plus the button `Alterar reserva` for the admin, which opens the project's `Alterar reserva` dialog; saving brings the row back (F5, F5b).
- `rename` opens from the ellipsis menu of a row (`Ações da unidade 905`, `Ações da área comum Hall`) or of the page (`Ações da torre`, `Ações do andar`, `Ações da unidade`, `Ações da área comum`; on a phone it sits in the back-link row, on a desktop beside the title): `Renomear` (`Renomear torre` / `Renomear andar` on those), the dialog `Renomear a unidade` with the field `Nome` (mono for a unit) and the hint `Único na torre.` (`Único na obra.`, `Único no andar.`), Enter or `Salvar` renames, an unchanged name just closes, a taken name shows the F4 sentence (R6, R7). The tower menu also has `Adicionar área comum`, which opens the field in `Áreas comuns da torre`. Every menu ends with a separator and the destructive `Remover` item (see `remove`); a floor and a unit have `Mover para cima` and `Mover para baixo` before it (see `move`).
- `move` swaps a floor or a unit with its neighbour of the same kind from the menu of a unit row, of the unit page or of the floor page: `Mover para cima` / `Mover para baixo` (for a floor, up is one floor higher; for a unit, one place earlier in the list, passing over the common areas between). At the top or at the end it does nothing; the list reorders in place and a failure toasts `Não foi possível mover. Confira sua conexão e tente de novo.` (not reachable on a healthy instance).
- `remove` is the menu item `Remover unidade` / `Remover andar` / `Remover torre` / `Remover área comum`, one action whose result the server decides. An empty leaf (a unit, a common area, a floor without units or areas, a tower without floors) goes at once with the toast `Unidade 903 excluída.` (`Área comum Hall excluída.`, `10º andar excluído.`, `Torre C excluída.`) and the button `Desfazer` for 6 seconds, which recreates it with the same id, place, typology, rooms, changes and disciplines (R1); a unit's or a common area's place goes back to the project's reservation and `Desfazer` takes it again, refused with `Não foi possível trazer a unidade 903 de volta: a reserva da obra acabou.` when the reservation was spent meanwhile (R1b, route-tree tests only). A floor or tower with children asks first in an alert dialog on a desktop and a bottom panel on a phone (`Excluir o 10º andar?`, `O 10º andar não tem pins, plantas nem dados. As 2 unidades e a área comum dele também são excluídas.`, `As 3 unidades voltam para a reserva da obra.`, counting the units and common areas, or `A área comum volta para a reserva da obra.` when a single common area is all it returns, `Cancelar` / `Excluir`) and then toasts `10º andar excluído.` with no `Desfazer` (R2). Removing the location whose page is open goes up to its parent. A failure keeps the location and toasts `Não foi possível remover a unidade 903. Confira sua conexão e tente de novo.` (R8, route-tree tests only).
- `duplicate` (R9a, R9b) opens from the same menus as `rename`: `Duplicar torre` on `Ações da torre`, `Duplicar andar` on `Ações do andar`, `Duplicar unidade` on `Ações da unidade 903` (a floor's unit row and the unit's own page); a common area has none. The dialog `Duplicar a Torre A` / `Duplicar o 9º andar` / `Duplicar a unidade 903` (a bottom panel on a phone) has the field `Nome` prefilled with the next free name (`Torre C`, the floor above the top numbered one such as `11º andar`, the next number on the floor `905`, mono) and under it `Usa 4 das 50 unidades que restam nesta obra. Depois, sobram 46.` (`Usa 1 da única unidade que resta nesta obra. Depois, sobram 0.` with one left), counting the units and common areas the copy creates. Enter or `Duplicar` sends one request: a tower copies its floors, units and common areas under the next tower, a floor goes on top of the tower with its units renumbered by the new number (`901` to `1301`, `9A` to `13A`; a unit not named after its floor, such as `Loja 1` on a `Térreo`, becomes `Loja 1 cópia 1` with the lowest number free in the tower) and its common areas, a unit goes to the end of its floor with the same typology and the same changes (rooms added, dropped, renamed). A copied common area, of a floor or of a tower, also follows the typology of its original with the same changes. Every copied location, the tower and each floor included, keeps the disciplines its original turned on or off. Toasts `Torre C criada.`, `11º andar criado.`, `Unidade 905 criada.`; a taken name shows the F4 sentence under `Nome` (`Já existe o 9º andar na Torre B.`). Over the reserva the line becomes a danger alert `Duplicar a Torre A usa 67 unidades e restam 49.` (`... e a reserva da obra acabou.` with none), `Duplicar` is disabled and the admin gets the button `Alterar reserva`, which opens its dialog over this one. Pins, plans and unit data are never copied (only structure exists today).
- `edit-delivered` shows no add row, no ellipsis menu (so no `Duplicar`) and no `Adicionar` on a delivered project; a write answered `PROJECT_DELIVERED` reloads the project and turns the page read-only (route-tree tests only).
- `unit-page` shows the `Tipologia` card (name, an outline badge `3 alterações` when it has changes, `Os cômodos acompanham a tipologia Tipologia A. ...`; without one `Sem tipologia` and `Os cômodos desta unidade são só dela.`), the `Cômodos` list with the count, `Só desta unidade` on the rooms only the unit has, and `Tirados desta unidade` with `Da tipologia Tipologia A` (U1, U2). The page menu has `Renomear`; a delivered project shows no button or menu.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Tap a project card on `/projects`, then a tower card, a floor label, a unit or a common area.
- Open `/projects/<id>` or `/projects/<id>/locations/<locationId>` directly.
- From a project's `Configuração da obra`, the `Estrutura` and `Tipologias` cards (`/projects/<id>/configuration`).

Select the back link with `{ name: "Torre B", exact: true }`; on `Residencial Jardins` Torre B, `Unidade 701` from the façade backs to `Torre B`, via `7º andar` it backs to `7º andar`. Both towers have a `701`, so reach it through the façade, not by a `locationId` query.
- **Not found.** `page.goto("/projects/<id>/locations/0190b000-0000-7000-8000-999999999999")` shows `Local não encontrado`; `Voltar para a obra` lands on the project.

## Gotchas

- For desktop scrolling proof, capture the wizard before and after scrolling, then repeat at 1280×600 with enough floors to overflow the preview. The preview and create button must stay inside the viewport with a 24px inset; scroll over the tower list to reach its lower floors without moving the page. Repeat at 1280×600 with `Tirar a regra 1` and a reservation below the plan: the untyped card and `Alterar reserva` push the preview past the viewport, so scrolling over its title scrolls the whole preview while the tower list keeps at least 180px. On a phone the header still scrolls and the four-step layout and bottom bar are unchanged.

- A menu stays open only until the screenshot: `shot` resizes the viewport height for a full-page capture, which closes a Radix menu (a dialog stays open), so press `Escape` after the shot and reopen the menu before the next click, and let the 150 ms open animation settle first.
- The inline field takes focus after the menu closes (the add forms) and the dialog's `Nome` field takes it on open: assert `toBeFocused()` rather than clicking into them.
- A dialog's `Duplicar` button is `getByRole("button", { name: "Duplicar", exact: true })`; the menu item has the kind in its name (`Duplicar andar`). Settle 300 ms after a menu closes or a toast shows before a `shot`, or the capture catches the fading menu behind the dialog.
- The `Adicionar` button of the home is `getByRole("button", { name: "Adicionar", exact: true })`; `Adicionar unidade` and `Adicionar área comum` are other buttons.
- When adding floors, `Do andar` also matches the rule fields `Do andar da regra 1`: use `{ name: "Do andar", exact: true }`.
- The wizard's range inputs are `Primeiro andar`, `Último andar` and `Finais` (one set per range, in reading order across towers; with several towers each has `Nome`); select them with `{ exact: true }`, since the rule's `Finais da regra 1` is a button, not a textbox, but `Primeiro andar` and the like can match more than once, and a range card is `getByRole("region", { name: "Faixa 2" })`. The floor fields take any text and refuse what is not a whole number from the 1º. The common area inputs are named `Área comum da obra`, `Área comum de cada torre` and, in the one-floor form, `Nome da área comum`, `Torre`, `Andar`, button `Adicionar ao andar`. Each add row also has `Tipologia da área comum` (`Sem tipologia` until one is chosen); a chosen one shows on the tag as `Salão de festas · Salão de festas`.
- `Alterar reserva` in the wizard is a button, not a link: it opens `getByRole("dialog", { name: "Alterar reserva" })` over the wizard, the draft stays, and on `Salvar` the alert recomputes from the refetched units.
- The wizard has several `Adicionar` buttons (project area, tower area): fill the field and press Enter instead of clicking by name. A tipologia's room is the combobox `Novo cômodo`; Enter adds, leaving the field does not. A rule is the card `getByRole("group", { name: "Regra 1" })` on both viewports; its controls are named `Tipologia da regra 1`, `Torre da regra 1`, `Do andar da regra 1` and `Até o andar da regra 1` (selects, `selectOption({ label: "3º" })`) and the menu button `Finais da regra 1`. The finals menu is a Radix menu: click the button, click the `menuitemcheckbox` (`01`, `Todos os finais`) as many times as needed, then press `Escape`; while it is open the button is hidden from `getByRole`, and `shot` closes it, so shoot after `Escape`.
- On a phone the wizard has no `Prévia` heading until the fourth step; select `Criar estrutura` only there.
- A full-page `shot` on a phone draws the fixed bottom bar in the middle of the image. To show the end of a step as the user sees it, scroll to the bottom and `shot(name, { screenOnly: true })`.

- Read the ids with `query`: they are generated on seed and change with every `up`.
- A unit's rooms and typology are seeded only on `Residencial Jardins` (types A, B, Cobertura, Duplex; it alone has common-area tipologias), `Edifício Aurora` (`Apartamento`), `Parque das Águas · Fase 2` (`Padrão`), `Residencial Vila Nova` and Vértice's `Residencial Jardins` (`Tipologia A`); the other projects have no typologies.
- A floor's unit link is named by its row text (`903 Tipologia A · 3 alterações`): select it with a regex such as `/^903/`.
- Two projects are named `Residencial Jardins` (Horizonte and Vértice): filter by organization or sign in as Ana, who sees only Horizonte's.
- The façade links are named by their label (`9º andar`, `Unidade 903`); the visible text of a floor is shortened (`9º`).
- No tower name appears anywhere in a single-tower project, so select its floors and units by name.
