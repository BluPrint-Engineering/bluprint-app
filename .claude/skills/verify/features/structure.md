# Project structure

A project's home at `/projects/<id>` shows its structure: a card per tower when there are several, the tower's façade directly when there is one, and the project's common areas. A tower at `/projects/<id>/locations/<towerId>` reads as its façade, one floor per row with the highest on top, units in columns aligned on the widest floor and common areas in a column of their own. A floor lists its units, each with its unit type and changes, and its common areas; a unit opens its unit type and its rooms, a common area opens its rooms. A blank project offers `Montar estrutura`, the wizard at `/projects/<id>/structure/new`: on a desktop one page with the towers, the common areas, the tipologias and a sticky preview; on a phone four steps with a bottom bar. Nothing is saved before `Criar estrutura`, which sends one request that creates the tipologias with their rooms and the towers, floors, units and common areas of the plan; each unit is born with the rooms of the tipologia its rules give it. Anyone linked to an in-progress project (and the organization's admin) adds and renames in place: `Adicionar` on the home, `Adicionar unidade` and `Adicionar área comum` at the end of a floor's lists, `Adicionar área comum` on a tower, and an ellipsis menu per row and per page with `Renomear`. A unit and a common area edit their rooms in place, and a unit changes or returns to its unit type. The configuration page at `/projects/<id>/configuration` sums the structure up and edits the project's tipologias. A delivered project is read-only for every role: no add row and no menu, and the lock line `A obra foi entregue: a estrutura não pode mais ser alterada.`; moving, removing and adding floors are not built yet.

## Sub-features

- `home-empty` shows `A obra ainda não tem estrutura` with `Monte torres, andares, unidades e áreas comuns de uma vez. Depois, tudo se ajusta na fachada.`, the link `Montar estrutura` and the ghost button `Adicionar uma torre por vez`, which turns into the `Nome da torre` field prefilled `Torre A` (E1); a delivered project without structure shows `A estrutura aparece aqui quando alguém da equipe montar a obra.` under the lock line and no link.
- `wizard-steps` opens `Montar estrutura` with the project name as back link and `<project> · nada é salvo antes de criar`: `Torres e andares` (towers stepper, first and last floor, units per floor, `Térreo sem unidades`, `Hall em cada andar`, numbering `101, 102` or `1A, 1B`; with several towers each gets a `Nome` and a new one copies the previous with the next letter), `Áreas comuns` (of the project, of each tower, of one floor), `Tipologias` and `Prévia`; on a phone `Passo 1 de 4` with a bar `Continuar · <next step>` and `Voltar` (W1, W2, W4, W8).
- `wizard-unit-types` (step 3, W3) starts with one `Tipologia A` with no rooms, applied to every final and floor by the rule `Regra 1` (`todos`, `Primeiro`, `Último`); each tipologia card has `Nome da tipologia`, its room count, room tags (`Tirar Sala da Tipologia A`) and `Novo cômodo da Tipologia A` + Enter (a repeated room answers `Esse cômodo já está na tipologia.`), and `Nova tipologia` adds the next free letter; `Onde se aplica` lists one rule per row on a desktop (columns `Torre` with several towers, `Finais`, `Do andar`, `Até o andar`, `Tipologia`) and one card per rule on a phone, `Regra` adds one, the first rule that matches a unit wins, a new tower copies the previous tower's rules and taking a tipologia out takes its rules.
- `wizard-preview` shows `1 torre · 4 andares · 12 unidades`, the common area count with the units of each tipologia (`5 áreas comuns · Tipologia A: 12`, `· Sem tipologia: 4`), a card `4 unidades ficam sem tipologia. Os cômodos delas se cadastram em cada uma.` when a rule leaves units out (W4), the line `Usa 12 das 20 unidades que restam nesta obra. Depois de criar, sobram 8.` and one small façade per tower (W4).
- `wizard-validation` shows `Informe o último andar.`, `Informe o nome da tipologia.`, `Já existe outra tipologia com esse nome.`, `Use números como 01, 03, ou escreva todos.` and the like under the fields (on a phone it returns to the step that holds the field), scrolls to the first wrong one and focuses it, and sends nothing (W5).
- `wizard-reserve` disables `Criar estrutura` when the plan has more units than remain: `Esta estrutura tem 48 unidades e a obra tem 20. Tire 28 unidades ou aumente a reserva.` with the button `Alterar reserva` for the admin, `... ou peça mais reserva ao admin da construtora.` for a manager or assistant; on a phone the alert rides in the bottom bar above the buttons (W4b, W4c).
- `wizard-create` creates the plan, returns to the project home with the toast `Estrutura criada: 1 torre e 12 unidades.` and the façade (W10); a created unit opens its tipologia and the rooms the tipologia listed; a failure keeps the draft with `Não foi possível criar a estrutura. Confira sua conexão e tente de novo. Nada foi salvo.` and a retry reuses the same ids (W7); `NO_UNITS_LEFT`, `LOCATION_NAME_TAKEN` and `UNIT_TYPE_NAME_TAKEN` show their alerts with the server's numbers and names (W7b). The failure paths are not reachable on a healthy instance; the web route-tree tests cover them.
- `wizard-delivered` shows the lock line and no wizard for a delivered project.
- `wizard-not-blank` sends `/projects/<id>/structure/new` on a project that already has structure to its home, replacing the history entry; after `Criar estrutura` the browser's Back does not reopen the wizard.
- `home-towers` shows `Torres` with a card per tower (Andares, Unidades, Áreas comuns, Tipologias: `Tipologia A, Tipologia B, Cobertura`, or `Sem tipologia`) and `Áreas comuns da obra` (E2); a delivered project adds the lock line (E5).
- `home-single-tower` shows `Fachada` and `Áreas comuns` with the tower's and the project's common areas, and the tower's name nowhere (E3).
- `tower-facade` shows the façade, `Áreas comuns da torre`, the band `Torre · 15 andares · 50 unidades` and the back link to the project (T1); a tower without floors shows `A Torre B ainda não tem andares` (T3); a delivered project adds the lock line (T6).
- `floor-lists` shows `Unidades`, each row with `Tipologia A`, `Tipologia A · 3 alterações` or `Sem tipologia`, `Áreas comuns do andar`, the band `Andar · Torre A · 4 unidades` and the back link to the tower (F1); with a single tower both drop the tower.
- `add-tower` adds one tower from `Adicionar uma torre por vez` (E1) or from the `Adicionar` menu of the home (`Torre`, `Área comum da obra`, `Montar com o assistente`; E2, and E3 beside the façade): the field `Nome da torre` suggests the first free `Torre <letter>` (`Torre C` with two towers, `Torre B` with one), Enter adds, Escape cancels, and a single-tower project then shows both towers as cards. `Montar com o assistente` is a link to the wizard, which sends a project that already has structure back home (`wizard-not-blank`).
- `add-unit` turns the last row of a floor's `Unidades` into the field `Adicionar unidade` with the next number prefilled (`905`; `1001` on an empty `10º andar`), the hint `Usa 1 das 12 unidades que restam nesta obra.` (`Usa 1 da única unidade que resta nesta obra.` with one), Enter adds at the end of the floor and Escape cancels (F2, F3); a taken name shows `Já existe a unidade 903 na Torre A.` in the field in place of the hint, `nesta torre` with a single tower (F4); a `NO_UNITS_LEFT` answer shows `A reserva da obra acabou.` in the field and the row turns into F5 once cancelled. Floors, common areas and towers never use the reserva.
- `add-common-area` adds `Adicionar área comum` to the obra, a tower or a floor, with an empty field and no reserva hint; a taken name reads `Já existe uma área comum Hall neste andar.`.
- `add-reserve-over` keeps `Adicionar unidade` visible and disabled when no unit remains, described by `A reserva da obra acabou. Fale com o admin da construtora.` for a manager or assistant and `A reserva da obra acabou.` plus the button `Alterar reserva` for the admin, which opens the project's `Alterar reserva` dialog; saving brings the row back (F5, F5b).
- `rename` opens from the ellipsis menu of a row (`Ações da unidade 905`, `Ações da área comum Hall`) or of the page (`Ações da torre`, `Ações do andar`, `Ações da unidade`, `Ações da área comum`; on a phone it sits in the back-link row, on a desktop beside the title): `Renomear` (`Renomear torre` / `Renomear andar` on those), the dialog `Renomear a unidade` with the field `Nome` (mono for a unit) and the hint `Único na torre.` (`Único na obra.`, `Único no andar.`), Enter or `Salvar` renames, an unchanged name just closes, a taken name shows the F4 sentence (R6, R7). The tower menu also has `Adicionar área comum`, which opens the field in `Áreas comuns da torre`.
- `edit-delivered` shows no add row, no ellipsis menu and no `Adicionar` on a delivered project; a write answered `PROJECT_DELIVERED` reloads the project and turns the page read-only (route-tree tests only).
- `unit-page` shows the `Tipologia` card (name, an outline badge `3 alterações` when it has changes, `Os cômodos acompanham a tipologia Tipologia A. ...`; without one `Sem tipologia` and `Os cômodos desta unidade são só dela.`), the `Cômodos` list with the count, `Só desta unidade` on the rooms only the unit has, and `Tirados desta unidade` with `Da tipologia Tipologia A` (U1, U2). The page menu has `Renomear`; a delivered project shows no button or menu.
- `unit-rooms` edits the rooms in place: each row has the menu `Ações do cômodo <nome>` with `Renomear` and, below a separator, `Remover desta unidade` (room of the unit type: it moves to `Tirados desta unidade` and counts as a change) or `Remover cômodo` (room only the unit has: deleted); the last row `Adicionar cômodo` turns into a form (`Adicionar`, `Cancelar`, Enter, Escape) that adds a room only this unit has (U3); a taken name answers `Já existe o cômodo Sala nesta unidade.` and an empty one `Informe o nome do cômodo.`; `Renomear` opens a dialog on a desktop and a bottom panel on a phone, renaming a room of the unit type only in this unit (an alteration); `Devolver` in `Tirados desta unidade` brings the room back.
- `unit-type-change` offers `Trocar tipologia` (U4): a dialog `Trocar a tipologia da 903` with a radio per tipologia (`3 cômodos`) and `Sem tipologia`, `Trocar` disabled until another is chosen; the unit starts over on the new tipologia's rooms, or keeps its current rooms as its own without one, with the toast `A unidade 903 agora segue a tipologia Tipologia B.` / `A unidade 903 ficou sem tipologia.`; `Voltar à tipologia` appears only with changes and undoes all three kinds at once, toast `A unidade 903 voltou à tipologia Tipologia A.`.
- `common-area-page` shows only the `Cômodos` card, no `Tipologia`, with the same menu and `Adicionar cômodo` (U5; the error reads `nesta área comum`); a delivered common area without rooms says `Nenhum cômodo cadastrado.`.
- `back-origin` sends the back link of a unit or a floor common area to the screen it was opened from: a unit tapped on a tower's façade shows `Torre B` and goes back to the tower, one opened from its floor shows `7º andar` and goes back to the floor and then the tower, and on a single-tower project home the façade's unit goes back to the project. The origin rides in the URL as `?from=<ancestor id>` (or `project`), survives a reload, and an absent or non-ancestor origin falls back to the structural parent.
- `location-band` shows a unit in mono with `Unidade · Torre A · 9º andar` and a common area with `Área comum · da obra`.
- `location-not-found` shows `Local não encontrado` with `Voltar para a obra` for a location that does not exist, belongs to another project or sits in a project the user cannot see (T5).
- `load-states` shows `Carregando estrutura`, `Não foi possível carregar a obra` / `o local` with `Tentar de novo`, and `Sem conexão.`. Not reachable on a healthy instance; the web route-tree tests cover them.

- `config-structure` (C1, C5, C6) shows the `Estrutura` card of the configuration (`2 torres · 25 andares · 70 unidades · 27 áreas comuns`, with `Abrir estrutura` to the project home and the sentence `A estrutura se edita onde se navega: …`); a project without structure reads `A obra ainda não tem estrutura.` with `Montar estrutura`; a delivered project keeps `Abrir estrutura`, drops the buttons and shows the lock line. The `Assistente` shortcut is hidden while the project has structure, because `/structure/new` sends such a project back to its home; it returns with the add-floors ticket (#161) and its `?tower` flow.
- `config-unit-types` (C2) lists each tipologia under `Tipologias` (`7 cômodos · 34 unidades`) with a menu `Ações da <tipologia>`: `Editar cômodos` opens the row in place with room tags (a new one ringed), `Novo cômodo`, the line `Salvar muda as 34 unidades da Tipologia A. 1 unidade com alterações mantém o que foi mudado nela.` and `Salvar` / `Cancelar`; saving toasts `Tipologia A salva. 34 unidades atualizadas.` A room name a unit already has as a renamed room (`Lavanderia` on 903) is refused with `A tipologia não pode ter o cômodo Lavanderia: …` and the draft is kept. `Renomear` answers `Já existe a tipologia … nesta obra.` for a taken name; `Nova tipologia` asks a name and opens the new row to fill; `Excluir tipologia` deletes one without units and toasts, and reads `Excluir (só sem unidades)`, disabled, for one with units. A delivered project lists them with no menu and the lock line.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Tap a project card on `/projects`, then a tower card, a floor label, a unit or a common area.
- Open `/projects/<id>` or `/projects/<id>/locations/<locationId>` directly.
- From a project's `Configuração da obra`, the `Estrutura` and `Tipologias` cards (`/projects/<id>/configuration`).

## Driving it with drive.ts

Preconditions:

- `instance.ts doctor` prints no `FAIL`.
- Run `.claude/skills/verify/journeys/structure-wizard.ts` with `--as admin` (builds `Galpão Logístico Sul`, which reserves 20 units, on the phone and `Villa Toscana`, 40, on the desktop) and with `--as admin-no-units` (Igor: Vértice's `Residencial Jardins` has structure, so its wizard redirects home; a 124-unit plan on Vértice's blank `Torre Ipê`, which reserves 120 with 0 free, is over the reservation); a run mutates the seed, so `down` and `up` before the next.
- Run `.claude/skills/verify/journeys/structure-configuration.ts` with `--as admin` on `Residencial Jardins` (edits `Tipologia A`, renames `Duplex`, creates and deletes `Studio`), `Galpão Logístico Sul` (C5), the delivered `Residencial Vila Nova` (C6) and `Condomínio Bela Vista`; it mutates the seed, so `down` and `up` before each device.
- Run `.claude/skills/verify/journeys/structure-rooms.ts` with `--as admin` on `Residencial Jardins`' unit 903, its `Salão de festas` and a unit of the delivered `Residencial Vila Nova`; it mutates 903, so `down` and `up` before the phone run and again before the desktop run (one `--device` per boot).
- Run `.claude/skills/verify/journeys/structure-edit.ts` with `--as admin` on one device at a time (it adds towers, units and areas and renames, so `down` and `up` between the phone and the desktop run); run `.claude/skills/verify/journeys/structure-reserve.ts` with `--as admin-no-units` (Igor on Vértice's `Residencial Jardins`, whose reservation is spent).
- Run `.claude/skills/verify/journeys/structure.ts` with `--as admin` (Ana sees every project); `--as manager` (Carla) reaches the projects she is on and gets `Obra não encontrada` elsewhere.

- **Home states.** `page.goto("/projects/" + id)` with the id from `SELECT id FROM project WHERE name = ... AND organization_id = ...` through `query`; assert the headings above.
- **Façade.** On a tower, `page.getByRole("list", { name: "Fachada da Torre A" })` has one `listitem` per floor, the first being `Cobertura`; `page.getByRole("link", { name: "Unidade 903" }).click()` opens the unit.
- **Back links.** From a floor `page.getByRole("link", { name: "Torre A" }).click()` returns to the tower; the first link of the band returns to the project. Select the back link with `{ name: "Torre B", exact: true }`; on `Residencial Jardins` Torre B, `Unidade 701` from the façade backs to `Torre B`, via `7º andar` it backs to `7º andar`. Both towers have a `701`, so reach it through the façade, not by a `locationId` query.
- **Not found.** `page.goto("/projects/<id>/locations/0190b000-0000-7000-8000-999999999999")` shows `Local não encontrado`; `Voltar para a obra` lands on the project.

## Gotchas

- A menu stays open only until the screenshot: `shot` takes a full-page capture that closes a Radix menu, so press `Escape` after the shot and reopen the menu before the next click, and let the 150 ms open animation settle first.
- The inline field takes focus after the menu closes (the add forms) and the dialog's `Nome` field takes it on open: assert `toBeFocused()` rather than clicking into them.
- The `Adicionar` button of the home is `getByRole("button", { name: "Adicionar", exact: true })`; `Adicionar unidade` and `Adicionar área comum` are other buttons.
- The wizard's inputs are `Primeiro andar`, `Último andar` and `Unidades por andar` (with several towers, one set per tower and `Nome`); the common area inputs are named `Área comum da obra`, `Área comum de cada torre` and, in the one-floor form, `Nome da área comum`, `Torre`, `Andar`, button `Adicionar ao andar`.
- `Alterar reserva` in the wizard is a button, not a link: it opens `getByRole("dialog", { name: "Alterar reserva" })` over the wizard, the draft stays, and on `Salvar` the alert recomputes from the refetched units.
- The wizard has several `Adicionar` buttons (project area, tower area, room of each tipologia): fill the field and press Enter instead of clicking by name. Rule fields are named `Finais da regra 1`, `Do andar da regra 1`, `Até o andar da regra 1`, `Tipologia da regra 1`, `Torre da regra 1` on a desktop; on a phone they are labelled `Finais`, `Do andar`, `Até o andar`, `Tipologia`, `Torre` inside the group `Regra 1` (a `fieldset`), so scope by `getByRole("group", { name: "Regra 1" })`.
- On a phone the wizard has no `Prévia` heading until the fourth step; select `Criar estrutura` only there.

- Read the ids with `query`: they are generated on seed and change with every `up`.
- A unit's rooms and unit type are seeded only on `Residencial Jardins` (types A, B, Cobertura, Duplex), `Edifício Aurora` (`Apartamento`), `Parque das Águas · Fase 2` (`Padrão`), `Residencial Vila Nova` and Vértice's `Residencial Jardins` (`Tipologia A`); the other projects have no unit types.
- A floor's unit link is named by its row text (`903 Tipologia A · 3 alterações`): select it with a regex such as `/^903/`.
- Two projects are named `Residencial Jardins` (Horizonte and Vértice): filter by organization or sign in as Ana, who sees only Horizonte's.
- The façade links are named by their label (`9º andar`, `Unidade 903`); the visible text of a floor is shortened (`9º`).
- No tower name appears anywhere in a single-tower project, so select its floors and units by name.
